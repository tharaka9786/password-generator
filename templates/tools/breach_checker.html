    How is Your Privacy Protected with k-Anonymity?

    When you audit a password, PassForge calculates its SHA-1 hash and sends only the first 5 characters to the HIBP API. The API returns thousands of hash suffixes that begin with those 5 characters. PassForge completes the matching entirely inside the Python backend. Your plaintext password and complete hash never leave your machine.
